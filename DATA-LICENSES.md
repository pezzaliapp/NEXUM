Files generated by NEXUM's tests and benchmarks (`tests/golden/`, `bench/`, `ui/tests/unit/*.json`, `docs/`) may quote
identifiers, names and values of the public sources of the worlds they were built from; those values stay under the
licence of their source.

The npm packages of the interface keep their own licences: the policy is in `ui/license-policy.json`, the check in
`ui/scripts/check-licenses.mjs` (run after the build), and the packages distributed in the site are listed with the
full text of their licences in `/THIRD-PARTY-NOTICES.txt`.
